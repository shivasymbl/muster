Asymbl Inc is the controller for Asymbl Muster. Questions go to muster@asymbl.com.

Muster reads free/busy and event times only to compute when a group overlaps. It does not sell personal data.

You can disconnect a calendar in Settings. You can also revoke Muster from your Google or Microsoft account.

Muster is hosted at muster.asymbl.app. Calendar access is read-only.
